About The Pick-Up Parlor

Ice Cream, Reimagined.

Welcome to The Pick-Up Parlor, your neighborhood scoop stop with a high-tech twist. We’re bringing you the joy of classic ice cream with the speed and simplicity of the future. Designed for the modern sweet tooth, our parlor runs on one simple idea: ice cream should be easy. No lines. No melting wait. Just tap, grab, and go. Whether you're on your lunch break, post-class, or en route to your next adventure, your favorite frozen treat is ready when you are — perfectly packed and waiting in our sleek pick-up pods.

    What We’re Serving: Handcrafted small-batch ice cream with bold, dreamy flavors Classic cones, and build-your-own sundaes.

    Seasonal drops + limited-time collabs to keep things extra sweet

    Why We're Different: We’re not a hangout spot. We’re not a sit-down scoop shop. We’re the in-and-out, sweet-and-done, order-ahead ice cream parlor of the future — for people who like their dessert as efficient as their caffeine fix.

    Our Mission: To deliver joy in every scoop, as fast as you need it — without cutting corners on flavor, fun, or freshness.

    So go ahead — place your order, swing by, and experience ice cream the way it should be: fast, fun, and always worth the pick-up.
